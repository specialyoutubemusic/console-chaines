Channel Production Console

A private, single-user tool for managing the owner's own YouTube music channels.

What it does

  • Sets the title, description, tags, category, language, thumbnail and scheduled publish time of videos on the owner's own channels.
  • Declares altered or synthetic content and "not made for kids" on each video, as YouTube requires.
  • Reads the owner's channel and video analytics to follow how the videos perform.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. The tool runs locally on the owner's computer; it is not offered to the public and does not act on anyone else's account.
